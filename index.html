About me

  • Second-year Electrical & Computer Engineering student at WPI. Before WPI I spent my last 2 years of high school at the Maine School of Science and Math (MSSM), a public boarding school in Limestone, ME.
  • I have worked in construction for Habitat for Humanity as well as deconstruction/material preservation at Second Story Builders, LLC.
  • Computer Science was my main interest for many years, then in high school I built a computer and became interested in computer engineering. At WPI I joined the WPI Wireless Association which is our club for Amateur Radio. Since then I have mainly been interested in RF engineering.
  • I have built many projects over the years mostly involving electronics and software. I started this website in the summer of 2026.
  • My hobbies include road cycling, cross-country skiing, a bit of running, and amateur radio (My callsign is KC1YKK).
  • Although I have spent a lot of my life so far in front of computers, I try to go outside and adventure and I wish I had done that more. The most time I spend outside is road cycling where I can go very far and explore. I have gone bikepacking/camping with amateur radio equipment.
