First trip

On 2026-8-17 I set out for a construction site with a concrete pad owned by someone Mom knew in Searsport who was building a house. I had just finished attatching stuff onto the bike and I really wanted to try bikepacking before heading to WPI. It went pretty good, I had to get used to the bike being really (top) heavy. I even brought my radio equipment and I set up an antenna at the site and was able to operate from inside my single-person tent. I didn't think I would need a pillow because it would be big which turned out to be stupid so I didn't sleep well. The next day I packed up and headed home.

Food: I packed some trail mix and bought canned food at the Belfast co-op.

Second trip, starting 2026-9-5

Labor day weekend I spent 3 days and 2 nights going 75 + 83 + 100 = 258 miles.

Day 1, 75 mi

I headed west out of worcester to the northern end of the Quabbin reservoir because it looks sparsely populated that way on the map. I liked the town of Petersham. I went through alot of farmland and backroads and got rained on, all of which was nice. I then continued northwest and crossed into Winchester NH on a flat pretty road. At some point I found a dam with some weird looking mechanical stuff (2nd photo). The connecticut river was also pretty. I crossed into Brattleboro, VT and stayed with a guy, Paul who I met on Warmshowers.

Day 2, 83 mi

Going south on the VT side of the river I saw alot of lumber yards. There was a dam/powerplant with an old turbine out front (2nd photo). Later on there was a "free stuff" stand on the side of the road with some books (along with other junk). Most of them were from the 1940s and 50s, I didn't keep any. Through MA it was mostly flat with some paved bike trails. In southern MA I entered urban hell with lots of intersections. Got to the 2nd house in CT in the afternoon. The owners texted me saying they wouldn't be there the whole time so I got some stranger's house to myself.

Day 3, century

Right at the start I felt like shit because my legs were getting sore and I hadn't eaten enough. I found a Subway 10 miles away on Apple Maps and made my way there first. I got whatever 1 ft long had the most calories from the menu, after which I felt better. Apple had me go pretty far south in CT to avoid hills. It then put me on rail trails for a long time. Most of the trails were good but some were too rocky for my thin tires and I turned around a few times and walked once. Once I got into the NW corner of RI it was getting late. I put the destination in Worcester into my phone and it sayed 25 miles, Strava already had 75 miles on it so I ended up doing a century. The last 25 miles went surprisingly well. I had stopped quite a bit during the day to eat junk food and I had plenty of calories in me. It got dark which was really fun. When I was riding into Worcester on the southern bike trail it was completely dark and a train was rumbling along next to me, fun times.
