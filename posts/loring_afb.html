Exploring Loring Air Force Base - Hadrian Ward

What is this place?

Loring AFB was an air base built in the town of Limestone in northeastern Maine. It was shut down in the '90s after the Cold War ended. It had a lot of nukes stored there ready to be flown over to Moscow at any moment (fun times). This page is mostly to show many photos I have from exploring the place over my 2 years at MSSM (and afterward) which is a 20 minute bike ride away.

Train coal dumping station

In the summer of 2026 I explored the base with my friend Brenden who guaduated MSSM sometime around 2000 (+/-). We spent a long time exploring every detail of the coal station among other parts of the base.

The Bunkers

There was a heavily guarded area to the northeast of the base with bunkers to store the nukes. About half of them are accessible by car and the other half are in the woods and gated off, although you could ride a bike around back there. Most of them look like industrial hobbit holes and you can go inside them. There are two other bunkers which look like regular buildings except the entire second stories are solid concrete with fake windows painted on them. One of them is a guard shack, which I have not been able to get in. The other is I believe the building where the nukes would be assembled before being moved down to the south end of the runway to be loaded onto airplanes.
